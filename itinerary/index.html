Veterans Day departure

Denmark, then Austria.

Leave Indianapolis on Wednesday 11 November 2026. Sleep beside Tivoli that first night in Copenhagen, sleep inside Tivoli on Friday, ride west to Billund for Saturday night, spend Sunday with LEGO, then base in Skjern until Thursday and fly out of Copenhagen Airport for Austria.

Two calendar facts to book around

  1. Tivoli Christmas opens Friday 13 November. Halloween ended 1 November. The gardens are shut Thursday 12 November — your first Copenhagen night — then reopen the evening you check into Nimb. Tivoli Food Hall stays open off the street in between.
  2. LEGOLAND Billund is closed 15 November. The park’s 2026 Christmas season starts 28 November. Stay at Hotel LEGOLAND Saturday as planned, but spend Sunday at LEGO House (open 10:00–16:00 that Sunday) instead of the outdoor park. Shift the whole trip two weeks later if the rides are non-negotiable.

Midweek base is Skjern, west Jutland — about 45 minutes west of LEGOLAND, hotel across from Skjern Station. Thursday’s train to Copenhagen is longer from here than from Aarhus: plan 4½–5½ hours plus the metro to CPH.
