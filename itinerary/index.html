Denmark, then Austria.

Leave Indianapolis on Wednesday 11 November 2026. Sleep beside Tivoli that first night in Copenhagen, sleep inside Tivoli on Friday, ride west to Billund for Saturday night, spend Sunday with LEGO, then base in Skejby until Thursday and fly out of Copenhagen Airport for Austria.

Two calendar facts to book around

  1. Tivoli Christmas opens Friday 13 November. Halloween ended 1 November. The gardens are shut Thursday 12 November — your first Copenhagen night — then reopen the evening you check into Nimb. Tivoli Food Hall stays open off the street in between.
  2. LEGOLAND Billund is closed 15 November. The park’s 2026 Christmas season starts 28 November. Stay at Hotel LEGOLAND Saturday as planned, but spend Sunday at LEGO House (open 10:00–16:00 that Sunday) instead of the outdoor park. Shift the whole trip two weeks later if the rides are non-negotiable.

“Skjeeb” is mapped as Skejby, the Aarhus N neighbourhood around the university hospital — Four Points Flex and GUESTapart are both there. If you meant Skive or Skjern, the Thursday airport run still works; only the midweek base changes.

    Where you sleep

    Thu 12 Nov

    Near Tivoli

    Andersen Boutique Hotel

    Helgolandsgade 12, Vesterbro. Five minutes on foot to the gardens and København H — right for a late arrival from CPH. Sister hotel Absalon is across the street if Andersen is full. Villa Copenhagen is the splurge, literally next to Tivoli.

    Helgolandsgade 12, 1653 København V

    Book Andersen Absalon Villa Copenhagen

    Fri 13 Nov

    On Tivoli’s grounds

    Nimb Hotel

    The Moorish palace actually inside the gardens, Bernstorffsgade 5. Stay includes garden entry and ride tickets when Tivoli is open — and Friday is opening night of Christmas. Tivoli Hotel & Congress Center is the more affordable Tivoli-branded backup (10–15 min walk, not inside the fence).

    Bernstorffsgade 5, 1577 København V

    Book Nimb Tivoli Hotel backup

    Sat 14 Nov

    At LEGOLAND

    Hotel LEGOLAND

    Aastvej 10, on the resort. Confirm they are taking 14 November guests — the advertised Christmas hotel packages run Thursday–Sunday from 26 November. If the hotel is dark, Lalandia Billund (year-round water park) is the practical on-site alternative.

    Aastvej 10, 7190 Billund

    Hotel LEGOLAND Lalandia Billund
